TJA採譜台 is a tool for making chart files in the TJA format. There is a browser version and an iPhone app, made by maosun-dev as an individual.

4. Information collected

The developer does not collect personal information such as your name, email address or location.

Browser version

Song and sound files are used only on your computer. They are never sent anywhere.

Your work-in-progress chart, settings and replaced hit sounds are saved only in that browser, for the next time you open it. You can delete them with your browser's "delete site data".

The visitor count at the bottom of the page is counted with an external service, Tally (tally.yuki.sh). Because of this, access records (such as your IP address) also reach Tally when you open the page.

Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, and the page itself from GitHub Pages. Because of this, access records (such as your IP address) also reach Google and GitHub. They are handled under their respective privacy policies (Google, GitHub).

iPhone app

Song and sound files, charts you make and settings are saved only on your iPhone and are not sent to the developer. Deleting the app deletes them too. Exported files are passed only to the destination you choose in the share screen.

5. Ads

In both versions, song and sound files and the charts you make are never sent for advertising.

Browser version

Google AdSense ads may be shown. How the ads work (cookies, etc.) and how to opt out are described in the site-wide privacy policy (Japanese).

iPhone app

Full-screen ads from Google "AdMob" are shown when you load a song, import a file and after exporting. For ads, the following information may be sent to Google.

  • Advertising identifier (IDFA) — only if you allow tracking
  • IP address
  • Device model and OS version
  • Records of ads viewed or tapped
  • Approximate region estimated from the IP address

You can change whether to allow tracking at any time in iPhone "Settings" → "Privacy & Security" → "Tracking".

For how Google handles information, see the following pages.

  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services
  • Google Privacy Policy
